Formatting Guide

To avoid database rejection, ensure your spreadsheet conforms to the structure below:

Header Column Expected Format
student_email Registered student email (lowercase)
date Commute date (YYYY-MM-DD)
status Commute Status (Present or Absent)
route_number_or_id Registered Route Bus Code (Optional)
Standard encoding is UTF-8. Non-matching commuter email accounts will log failure alerts in notifications.
